Create one photorealistic virtual try-on image.

INPUT IMAGES
- Image 1: the original clothing / shopping image selected by the user.
- Images 2 onward: reference photos of the user. They show the same person and establish their identity, face, body proportions, skin tone, and hair.

TASK
Show the person from the reference photos wearing the clothing visible in Image 1. Transfer the clothing to the user; do not copy the identity or body of a model in the original image. But keep the pose and bg of the model, and not of the reference images.

PRESERVE
- The user's recognizable face, age, body proportions, skin tone, hairstyle, and distinguishing features.
- The clothing's cut, color, pattern, material, texture, details, and visible branding.
- Realistic fabric drape, fit, seams, lighting, shadows, and anatomical proportions.
- The model's bg, pose.

COMPOSITION
Frame the person so the selected clothing is clearly visible. If the original shows only one clothing item, preserve the user's other clothing where possible. If it shows a complete outfit, transfer the complete visible outfit. If clothing details are hidden, infer them conservatively. But be honest to the user's face, features and body proportions.

OUTPUT
Return a single finished photograph. No comparison layout, text, watermarks, interface elements, extra people, or duplicated body parts.
